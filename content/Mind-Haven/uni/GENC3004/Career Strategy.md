### High inflation
Businesses expect costs to rise so they keep increasing prices. People expect cost of living to rise, so they keep asking for pay rises, independent of productivity which increases costs to business so business increase prices to recapture profit margins in a Wage Price Spilral

Bad for economy :- cost of living for vulnerable, uncertainty for businesses, encourages borrowing, discourages saving, can create speculative bubbles, decreases home affordability.

**Response to high inflation**
Decreases new home construction, decreases business investment, increases home loan payments, increases home rent payments, increases income retirees

### Monetary and Fiscal Policy
Monetary Policy (Interest Rates)
	Reserve Bank of Australia
	Higher rates slow things down
	Lower rates not very effective at speeding things up

Fiscal Policy (Government Expenditure and Taxation)

## Developing your career strategy

Labour market
	Define your labour market in which you plan to compete - Type of work, industry and geographic location
Economic cycle
	How would a boom or recession affect employment in your labour market - Is employment sensitive to the state economy or unrelated? Why ?
	Identify the current phase of the economy - Boom, normal, recession
	How do you believe the phase is affecting income and employment?
Structural change
	How will it affect income and employment over the next 10 years ? - from changing technology (4th industry revolution) and other factors (post-pandemic)
	How will it change the specific work activities that you expect to perform ?
Values
	Explain the link between your chosen labour market and work activities and your values and principles
Personality
	How does your career link with your intelligences and personality - Consider the Big FIVE, MBTI, and others
Work styles
	How does your career link with your skills, experiences and work styles ? - Consider your DELIs
Career goals
	Briefly define some specific career goals for each of your life stages
		1. Type of company
		2. Job role or position
		3. Expected income
Career strategy
	What specific steps will you take to achieve career goals ?
		Workplace experiences
		Developing new skills
		Regular mentoring
		Network building
		Active 'marketing' of your skills via social media
		Additional part-time study (such as an MBA with AGSM)
Risk analysis
	Identify some risks associated with achieving your career goals - How likely are they to occur and what will be the impact ?
	What steps can you take to manage the risk ? MEAT: Mitigate, Eliminate, Accept or Transfer
Career action plan
	What specific actions do you need to take
		Short-term (1 year)
		Medium-term (1 to 5 years)
		Long-term (5+ years)
